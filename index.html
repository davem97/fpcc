FPSCharController

A custom and robust, easy-to-use first-person character controller aimed at improving the standard in-built Unity component.

Western Weapons System

A weapon and ballistic system focusing on Wild West era firearms with hipfire, aiming, recoil, and recoil recovery features.

Inventory & Crafting System

A flexible grid-based inventory framework complete with drag-and-drop item management, stacking, and crafting recipes.

Modular Quest Manager

Easily create branching storylines, track objectives, handle dialogue triggers, and reward players with a node-based workflow.

Dynamic Audio Manager

Control 3D spatial sounds, ambient audio zones, music crossfading, and sound mixer groups cleanly through a centralized singleton.

Behavior tree-driven enemy AI featuring line-of-sight detection, cover usage, tactical flanking behaviors, and melee/ranged attacks.
